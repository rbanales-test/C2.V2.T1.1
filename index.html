Harmonic Stacker Simulator

⚠️ Volume Warning / Cuidado con el Volumen

This simulator synthesizes raw acoustic overtones and inharmonic frequencies that can produce sharp, high-pitched spikes. Please lower your headphone or system volume before starting.

Objective: Experiment directly with fundamental frequencies, harmonic overtones, and inharmonic overtones on pitch B4 (494 Hz) to observe how changing harmonic ratios alters perceived timbre.

Instructions & Accessibility:

  1. Click Start Simulator to initialize the engine (integrated safety limiter active).
  2. Press Spacebar or click Play Audio at any time to toggle the sound.
  3. Use Tab to move between sliders and arrow keys (← →) to adjust values.
  4. Complete the 5 sequential missions to unlock free exploration.
  5. Notice how odd harmonics sound like a clarinet, adding even harmonics creates a saxophone body, and inharmonic overtones produce a bell-like tone.
