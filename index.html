Harmonic Stacker Simulator

Objective: Experiment directly with fundamental frequencies, harmonic overtones, and inharmonic overtones to observe how changing harmonic ratios alters perceived timbre.

Instructions:

  1. Click Start Simulator and turn on the audio.
  2. Complete the 5 sequential missions to unlock the controls.
  3. Use the Tab key or your mouse to adjust the sliders.
  4. Notice how odd harmonics sound like a clarinet, adding even harmonics creates a saxophone tone, and inharmonic overtones produce a bell-like sound.
